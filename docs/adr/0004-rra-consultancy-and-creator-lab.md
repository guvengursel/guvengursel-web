# ADR 0004: Endorse River Runs Again as the Consultancy and Creator Lab Brand

- Status: Accepted by principal direction; commercial activation remains gated
- Date: 2026-08-01
- Decision owner: Güven Gürsel (Kaptan)
- Supersedes: the brand-role allocation in ADR 0002
- Does not supersede: preservation, release-authority, evidence, or source-boundary controls

## Context

ADR 0002 endorsed a dual-brand portfolio in which guvengursel.com carried professional consultancy and River Runs Again carried creator-facing work. Kaptan subsequently clarified that River Runs Again must combine both enterprise AI consultancy and the creator lab, while guvengursel.com remains the founder and personal trust surface.

The initial consultancy focus is Upper Austria organizations with roughly 50–500 employees, international operations, and material Microsoft 365 usage. Entry may originate in any department. The intended commercial path is workshop-led discovery followed by optional department-specific assistants, agents, workflows, pipelines, implementation, and maintenance.

The model must remain compatible with a measured solopreneur path, explicit IT/security co-ownership, EU data-protection and AI-governance constraints, and Kaptan's external-action gates.

## Decision drivers

1. One visible brand must own the full consultancy promise and its proof.
2. The creator lab should strengthen enterprise credibility without obscuring the buying path.
3. Güven's operating history and judgement must remain visible and attributable.
4. Departmental entry must stay flexible while engagement governance stays explicit.
5. Internal IT or the incumbent MSP must be treated as a co-owner, not an obstacle.
6. Commercial claims must be earned through measured, permissioned evidence.
7. The first offer must be repeatable before an offer catalogue is created.

## Considered options

### Option A — Preserve ADR 0002 role allocation

- guvengursel.com owns consultancy.
- River Runs Again owns creator work.

Rejected because it separates the service from the experimental proof that should support it and creates two competing consultancy narratives.

### Option B — Move all identity into River Runs Again

- River Runs Again owns consultancy and creator work.
- guvengursel.com becomes a redirect or disappears.

Rejected because it removes the clearest founder-authorship and trust surface.

### Option C — One endorsed RRA brand with two doors

- River Runs Again owns consultancy and creator-lab work.
- guvengursel.com remains the founder/personal trust surface and endorses RRA.
- RRA presents a consultancy-first organizational path and a separate Lab path.

Accepted.

## Decision

### Brand structure

River Runs Again is one endorsed brand with two distinct navigation and conversion paths:

1. **For Organizations** — AI workflow consultancy: discover, enable, implement, and maintain.
2. **The Lab** — creator work, synthetic demonstrators, field notes, and transparent learning.

The organizational path is primary on commercial RRA surfaces. The Lab supports proof and thought leadership but does not compete with the commercial call to action.

guvengursel.com remains the visible founder, operating-history, authorship, and trust surface. It routes appropriate professional interest to River Runs Again rather than running a competing consultancy offer.

### Engagement governance

Any department may initiate an engagement. Before paid discovery advances, three responsibilities must be named:

1. a budget owner;
2. a workflow owner;
3. an IT/security co-owner.

The responsibilities may be held by two people, but none may be absent.

RRA augments internal IT or the incumbent MSP. It does not bypass them, resell licenses by default, replace them, or create shadow infrastructure.

### Initial offer architecture

The initial commercial entry product is one modular **AI Workflow Discovery Workshop**. Foundation and Advanced content are selected through intake rather than sold as a catalogue of disconnected workshops.

A readiness sprint, implementation, or maintenance engagement may follow only as a separately scoped decision and contract. A workshop may validly conclude that no implementation should proceed.

### Evidence and risk posture

- Synthetic or public data is used for internal proof.
- Customer outcomes are claimed only after measurement and permission.
- Anonymous or aggregated employee discovery is the default.
- Individual performance scoring is excluded.
- Customer data is processed only within customer-approved systems and terms.
- High-risk employment, credit, biometric, and essential-service decision systems are outside the initial offer.
- Human authority, logging, tests, handover, and rollback are required for implementation.

### Activation boundary

This ADR authorizes planning artifacts and authority reconciliation only. It does not authorize outreach, publication, invoicing, registration, purchasing, contracts, production access, customer-data processing, or company formation.

The commercial-operability gate in the RRA consultancy business plan must pass before paid delivery or invoicing.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One brand owns the complete service and proof loop.
- The founder remains visible without creating a competing offer.
- Departmental entry remains flexible.
- IT alignment becomes part of the product instead of late-stage friction.
- The workshop creates a bounded path to evidence and optional implementation.

### Negative

- RRA must keep the consultancy and Lab paths visually and verbally distinct.
- guvengursel.com requires updated routing and cannot retain a duplicate consultancy funnel.
- Existing portfolio documents that encode the old role allocation require supersession notices or revision.
- The dual-purpose brand raises a clarity risk that must be tested at the R2 five-second comprehension gate.

### Risks and mitigations

- **Risk:** RRA appears as an unfocused creative studio.  
  **Mitigation:** consultancy-first information architecture and separate Lab navigation.

- **Risk:** RRA appears as a fictional multi-person agency.  
  **Mitigation:** visible "by Güven Gürsel" endorsement and explicit solopreneur operating model.

- **Risk:** Departmental buyers attempt to bypass IT.  
  **Mitigation:** IT/security co-owner is mandatory before discovery advances.

- **Risk:** Workshop marketing implies guaranteed savings.  
  **Mitigation:** sell the method and deliverables; publish outcomes only after evidence and permission.

- **Risk:** Company and invoicing assumptions are based on a VAT threshold.  
  **Mitigation:** separate AMS, Gewerbe, VAT, income-tax, SVS, insurance, and contract decisions at R1.
